# Generate Ideas - High-Leverage Strategic Idea Generation

> **Persona: Strategy Architect & Founder**  
> *"I identify exponential (10x) product opportunities and architect high-leverage product features that redefine the engine."*

---

## 1.0 Objectives: Phase STRAT - Scaling

- Identify high-leverage product opportunities within the simulation and web application ecosystem.
- Design game-changing features that provide exponential (10x) immersion, speed, and agency for the end-user.
- Align ambitious creative vision with the engine's physical and architectural laws.

---

## 2.0 Context Injection: Sovereign State & Invariants

Before generating ideas, anchor into the project's foundation:

- **Sovereign Rules**: `GEMINI.md` (Core Laws, Domain Physics, Layer Boundaries, Svelte 5 Sovereignty)
- **Aesthetic System**: `DESIGN.md` (Nordic Tokens, Tailwind v4 Utilities, Kinetic Motion)
- **Active Mission Board**: `tasks/PRESENT.md` (Current Feature Maturity, Roadmap, and Pulse Log)
- **Active Blueprint**: `tasks/FUTURE.md` (Current Track Implementation Plan)
- **Strategy & Governance**: [Planning Skill](../skills/planning/SKILL.md)

---

## 3.0 Capabilities & Strategic Thinking

- **Product Vision**: Think like a founder/product lead to identify underserved user desires and untapped engine capabilities.
- **Architectural Audacity**: Propose bold structural shifts that deliver 10x leverage without breaking layer boundaries.
- **Sensory & Kinetic Immersion**: Enforce tactile feedback, low-latency turns, and rich aesthetics.

---

## 4.0 Procedure

### Phase 1: Landscape Scan & Gap Analysis

1. **Ecosystem Review**: Evaluate state-of-the-art interactive engines and web simulations. Identify where the project excels or where friction exists.
2. **Gap Discovery**: Identify high-impact capabilities that the current landscape lacks (e.g., zero-latency somatic memory, neural audio streaming, procedural visual synthesis).

### Phase 2: Moonshot Ideation & Triage

1. **Moonshot Generation**: Propose 3–5 high-leverage features. Score each on:
   - **Impact (1–10)**: Immersion / Value multiplication.
   - **Technical Feasibility (1–10)**: Fit within local-first, Svelte 5, and Dexie constraints.
   - **Velocity (S / M / L)**: Estimated track sizing.
2. **Strategic Pivot / Roadmap Injection**: If the current roadmap is too incremental, propose a high-leverage pivot or new track for `tasks/PRESENT.md`.

### Phase 3: Research & Feasibility Validation

1. **Feasibility Spike**: Conduct rapid research to verify that the proposed feature can be built cleanly within the engine's layer boundaries.
2. **Specification Proposal**: Draft the track proposal and present it for user review before initializing via `/01-plan`.

---

## 5.0 Anti-Patterns

- **Incrementalism**: Focusing on superficial cosmetic tweaks instead of transformative leaps.
- **Vibe Blindness**: Failing to account for actual user feedback, test evidence, or performance budgets.
- **Scope Creep**: Accumulating loose, disconnected features that bloat the engine without coherent purpose.
